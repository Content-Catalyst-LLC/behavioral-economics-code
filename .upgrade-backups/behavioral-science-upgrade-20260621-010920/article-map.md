# Behavioral Economics Article Map

Companion repository for behavioral economics, bounded rationality, prospect theory, heuristics, time preference, social preferences, public policy, digital platforms, sustainability, and institutional design.

## Articles

- [Bounded Rationality in Economic Decision-Making](articles/bounded-rationality-economic-decision-making/) — How limited information, attention, computation, and time shape economic choice.
- [Prospect Theory and the Psychology of Risk](articles/prospect-theory-psychology-of-risk/) — Reference dependence, value functions, probability weighting, gains, and losses.
- [Loss Aversion and Risk Perception](articles/loss-aversion-risk-perception/) — Why losses often feel more powerful than equivalent gains.
- [Expected Utility and Its Behavioral Critique](articles/expected-utility-theory-rational-choice/) — The classical rational-choice benchmark and behavioral critiques.
- [Heuristics and Biases in Economic Decision-Making](articles/heuristics-biases-economic-decision-making/) — Mental shortcuts, cognitive limits, and systematic decision errors.
- [Anchoring Effects in Economic Judgment](articles/anchoring-bias-economic-judgment/) — How initial numbers and salient references shape estimates and choices.
- [Availability Bias and Economic Perception](articles/availability-bias-economic-perception/) — How vivid, recent, and memorable information shapes perceived likelihood and risk.
- [Framing Effects in Consumer Choice](articles/framing-effects-consumer-choice/) — How equivalent information changes decisions depending on presentation.
- [Status Quo Bias and Institutional Inertia](articles/status-quo-bias-institutional-inertia/) — Why existing arrangements often persist.
- [Time Discounting and Long-Term Decision-Making](articles/time-discounting-long-term-decision-making/) — How people weigh present and future outcomes.
- [Present Bias and the Psychology of Immediate Reward](articles/present-bias-immediate-reward/) — Why immediate costs and benefits dominate long-term welfare.
- [Mental Accounting in Personal Finance](articles/mental-accounting-personal-finance/) — Psychological accounts that shape spending, saving, and investment.
- [Self-Control and Commitment Devices in Behavioral Economics](articles/self-control-commitment-devices-behavioral-economics/) — Precommitment, temptation, procrastination, and long-term goals.
- [Behavioral Finance](articles/behavioral-finance/) — Investor psychology, market anomalies, herding, and risk perception.
- [Behavioral Finance and Investor Psychology](articles/behavioral-finance-investor-psychology/) — Investor behavior, overreaction, underreaction, and market decisions.
- [Overconfidence and Market Behavior](articles/overconfidence-bias-financial-markets/) — Excess confidence, forecasting error, trading, and risk-taking.
- [Herd Behavior in Financial Markets](articles/herd-behavior-financial-markets/) — Imitation, cascades, bubbles, and collective behavior under uncertainty.
- [Fairness, Reciprocity, and Social Preferences](articles/fairness-reciprocity-economic-behavior/) — How fairness and reciprocity shape behavior beyond self-interest.
- [Inequality Aversion in Economic Decision-Making](articles/inequality-aversion-economic-decision-making/) — Responses to unequal outcomes and perceived injustice.
- [Trust and Cooperation in Economic Systems](articles/trust-cooperation-economic-systems/) — Trust, cooperation, reputation, and the social foundations of exchange.
- [Nudge Theory and Behavioral Public Policy](articles/nudge-theory-behavioral-public-policy/) — Small changes in choice architecture that influence behavior.
- [Choice Architecture and Decision Environments](articles/choice-architecture-decision-environments/) — Defaults, salience, simplification, feedback, friction, and design.
- [Behavioral Regulation and Institutional Design](articles/behavioral-regulation-institutional-design/) — Behaviorally realistic regulation, compliance, and institutional design.
- [Behavioral Insights in Environmental Policy](articles/behavioral-insights-environmental-policy/) — Climate communication, conservation, risk perception, and energy behavior.
- [Behavioral Economics and Sustainable Consumption](articles/behavioral-economics-sustainable-consumption/) — Consumption habits, defaults, norms, pricing, and sustainable behavior change.
- [Behavioral Economics and Digital Platforms](articles/behavioral-economics-digital-platforms/) — Platform choice architecture, attention, personalization, and behavioral data.
- [Behavioral Design in Technology Systems](articles/behavioral-design-technology-systems/) — Interface design, defaults, friction, dark patterns, and ethical design.
- [Behavioral Economics in Organizational Decision-Making](articles/behavioral-economics-organizational-decision-making/) — Bias, incentives, group decision-making, and organizational behavior.
- [The Future of Behavioral Economics in Governance and Policy](articles/future-behavioral-economics-governance-policy/) — Behavioral economics in institutions, technology, regulation, and sustainability.
- [Behavioral Economics and Administrative Burden](articles/behavioral-economics-administrative-burden/) — Paperwork, complexity, stigma, friction, and public-service access.
- [Behavioral Economics and AI-Driven Choice Architecture](articles/behavioral-economics-ai-driven-choice-architecture/) — Algorithmic personalization, predictive nudges, autonomy, and governance.
- [Behavioral Economics and Climate Risk Perception](articles/behavioral-economics-climate-risk-perception/) — Salience, discounting, uncertainty, and climate-related decisions.
- [Behavioral Economics and Public Trust](articles/behavioral-economics-public-trust/) — Trust, legitimacy, compliance, communication, and behavioral interventions.
- [Behavioral Economics and Inequality](articles/behavioral-economics-inequality/) — Scarcity, stress, cognitive bandwidth, and the ethics of intervention.
- [Behavioral Economics and Dark Patterns](articles/behavioral-economics-dark-patterns/) — Manipulative design, opt-out difficulty, subscription traps, and consumer protection.
- [Loss Aversion: Why Losses Matter More Than Gains](articles/loss-aversion-losses-matter-more-than-gains/) — Expanded treatment of loss aversion and reference dependence.
