# Ethics and Scope

This repository is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, reproducible article support, and public-interest learning.

It should not be used for covert behavioral targeting, psychological profiling, manipulative interface design, dark-pattern optimization, predatory pricing, discriminatory policy targeting, or individual-level welfare decisions.

Behavioral economics can clarify human decision environments, but it can also be misused. The examples here should be interpreted through autonomy, transparency, fairness, reversibility, proportionality, and institutional legitimacy.
