# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without household covariates.
2. Compare segmented accounts, integrated prompts, and unified money views.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by savings-label strength quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by emergency-risk quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by debt-burden quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by present-bias quartile.
7. Vary windfall-spending assumptions.
8. Vary savings-reallocation assumptions.
9. Vary protected-liquidity assumptions.
10. Vary credit-card interest-rate assumptions.
11. Evaluate debt reduction and liquidity preservation separately.
12. Evaluate resilience index under alternative welfare weights.
13. Test whether integrated prompts reduce inefficiency without excessively reducing liquidity.

## External validity

Real-world mental-accounting effects depend on income stability, debt cost, household emergency risk, access to credit, financial literacy, app design, institutional trust, family obligations, public benefits, cultural norms, and the ability to revise account labels.
