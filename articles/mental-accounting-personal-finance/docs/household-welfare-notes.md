# Household Welfare and Mental Accounting Notes

Mental accounting should be evaluated by welfare, not by narrow fungibility alone. Some departures from fungibility protect liquidity, self-control, and dignity. Others create unnecessary interest costs or obscure total exposure.

A stylized household welfare expression is:

```text
Household Welfare = Net Worth + Liquidity + Goal Progress - Financial Cost - Behavioral Burden - Hardship Risk
```

## Key welfare questions

- Does the account label protect a goal the household endorses?
- Does it preserve needed liquidity?
- Does it create unnecessary high-interest debt persistence?
- Does it reduce stress or create rigidity?
- Does it help households see total assets and liabilities?
- Does the institution benefit from the household's segmentation?
- Is the account boundary transparent, reversible, and user-aligned?

## Recommended diagnostics

- Windfall consumption.
- Windfall debt repayment.
- Savings used for debt.
- Remaining high-interest debt.
- Remaining liquid savings.
- Annual interest cost.
- Debt-savings inefficiency gap.
- Financial resilience index.
- Heterogeneity by savings-label strength.
- Heterogeneity by liquidity need and emergency risk.
