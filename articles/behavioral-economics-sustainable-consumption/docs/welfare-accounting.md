# Welfare Accounting Framework

A policy comparison should distinguish:

1. Private welfare changes.
2. Environmental external benefits.
3. Fiscal cost.
4. Producer and supplier responses.
5. Distributional incidence.
6. Administrative burden.
7. Autonomy and transparency concerns.
8. Persistence of behavior change.

A simplified social welfare expression can be written as:

\[
W = \sum_i \omega_i \Delta U_i + \sum_i E_i - C_f - C_a
\]

where \(\omega_i\) represents distributional weights, \(\Delta U_i\) is private welfare change, \(E_i\) is environmental external benefit, \(C_f\) is fiscal cost, and \(C_a\) is administrative or implementation cost.

The synthetic workflows in this repository demonstrate this accounting structure without claiming empirical validity.
