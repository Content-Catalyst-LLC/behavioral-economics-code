# Data Dictionary

## household_id
Synthetic household identifier.

## period
Time period in panel simulations.

## income
Synthetic annual household income.

## income_quintile
Income-group classification used for distributional incidence.

## environmental_concern
Latent environmental preference or concern index.

## present_bias
Present-focused weighting parameter.

## loss_aversion
Loss-aversion parameter.

## norm_sensitivity
Responsiveness to social-norm information.

## friction_sensitivity
Sensitivity to administrative, search, effort, and switching costs.

## quality_uncertainty
Perceived uncertainty about sustainable option quality.

## policy_regime
Policy scenario: information_only, green_default, subsidy, subsidy_plus_default, regulation_plus_support.

## default_green
Indicator for whether the sustainable option is the default.

## subsidy
Reduction in the price premium of the sustainable option.

## norm_signal
Perceived share of peers adopting or supporting the sustainable option.

## adoption_probability
Modeled probability of sustainable-option adoption.

## adopted
Synthetic adoption outcome.

## private_welfare
Modeled private welfare under chosen option.

## external_benefit
Estimated social/environmental benefit per adoption.

## fiscal_cost
Public cost associated with subsidy or policy support.

## total_welfare
Private welfare plus external benefit minus fiscal cost.
