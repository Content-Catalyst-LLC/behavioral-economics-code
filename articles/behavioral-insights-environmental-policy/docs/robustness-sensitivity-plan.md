# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Professional environmental policy workflows should include robustness checks.

## Recommended checks

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare OLS, logit/probit for binary adoption, and OLS welfare models.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by income quintile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by energy-burden quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by present-bias quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by institutional-trust quartile.
7. Test alternative environmental-benefit weights.
8. Test alternative administrative-cost assumptions.
9. Test alternative fiscal-cost assumptions.
10. Compare adoption effects with welfare effects.
11. Report standard errors and confidence intervals where packages are available.

## Placebo checks for real data

- Pre-treatment adoption or enrollment.
- Baseline energy consumption.
- Prior program awareness.
- Baseline trust or environmental concern.
- Pre-policy trend in energy use or program applications.

## External validity

Real environmental policy effects depend on housing stock, local energy prices, climate zone, infrastructure, contractor availability, trust, income, tenure, public administration, and political context. Synthetic results should not be interpreted as real-world estimates.
