# Responsible Use

This scaffold supports synthetic-data research and reproducible learning about behavioral environmental policy.

Appropriate uses include:

- applied environmental economics teaching
- policy-evaluation methods demonstration
- welfare-analysis prototyping
- environmental program design research
- administrative burden analysis
- distributional incidence analysis
- reproducible documentation practice

Inappropriate uses include:

- manipulating public consent
- greenwashing
- obscuring policy costs
- targeting vulnerable households
- replacing structural climate policy with messaging
- production deployment without legal, ethical, distributional, and empirical review

Behavioral environmental policy should support transparency, fairness, accessibility, institutional accountability, environmental integrity, and public welfare.
