# Disposition Effect Notes

The disposition effect describes the tendency to sell winning assets too early and hold losing assets too long.

## Behavioral mechanism

- Purchase price becomes a reference point.
- Selling a loser realizes a loss.
- Holding a loser preserves the possibility of breaking even.
- Selling a winner locks in a gain.
- Realized gains provide psychological closure.

## Empirical cautions

Observed holding behavior may also reflect:

- tax-loss timing
- transaction costs
- liquidity needs
- beliefs about mean reversion
- private information
- portfolio constraints
- financial advice
- attention and monitoring frequency

## Research workflow

A robust workflow should compare:

- winner sale rates
- loser sale rates
- return momentum controls
- tax-period effects
- investor heterogeneity
- account-level goals
- realized versus unrealized gain/loss status
