# Policy Transition and Sustainability Notes

Loss aversion is central to sustainability because many environmental transitions require immediate visible costs in exchange for long-term collective benefits.

## Transition loss channels

- higher prices
- stranded assets
- job loss
- regional identity loss
- reduced convenience
- changed routines
- regulatory compliance
- loss of fossil-fuel rents
- changed land use
- infrastructure disruption

## Hidden losses from inaction

- climate damage
- biodiversity decline
- public-health harm
- heat exposure
- displacement
- water insecurity
- soil loss
- infrastructure failure
- intergenerational loss

## Design principles

- map losses explicitly
- compensate genuine transition burdens
- avoid protecting unjust privilege
- make hidden environmental losses visible
- use phase-ins and rebates where appropriate
- include affected communities
- pair urgency with agency
- evaluate distributional effects
