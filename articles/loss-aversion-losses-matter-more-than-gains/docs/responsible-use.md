# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include behavioral-economics teaching, behavioral-finance research, consumer-protection analysis, labor-market transition analysis, policy-loss mapping, sustainability governance, digital-platform accountability, institutional decision review, and synthetic simulation.

Inappropriate uses include manipulative fear framing, dark-pattern cancellation design, deceptive scarcity messages, exploitative subscription design, predatory investment-interface design, anti-worker wage framing, or using behavioral insights to bypass informed judgment.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as legal advice, financial advice, investment advice, labor advice, public-policy advice, or empirical claims about real consumers, workers, investors, platforms, firms, public agencies, communities, or institutions.
