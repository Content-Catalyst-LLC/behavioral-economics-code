# Digital Platform Loss-Framing Notes

Digital platforms can use loss framing to retain users, increase purchase likelihood, or intensify engagement.

## Common platform loss frames

- "You will lose access"
- "Your discount expires"
- "Only one left"
- "You are about to lose your streak"
- "Cancel and lose benefits"
- "Downgrade and lose features"
- "Missed opportunity"
- "Abandoned cart savings"
- "Limited-time bonus"
- "Points expiring soon"

## Governance questions

- Is the loss real?
- Is the loss exaggerated?
- Does the user have clear alternatives?
- Can the user easily exit?
- Is the message personalized to exploit vulnerability?
- Does the platform benefit from confusion?
- Are long-term costs hidden?

Responsible design uses loss warnings to inform, not manipulate.
