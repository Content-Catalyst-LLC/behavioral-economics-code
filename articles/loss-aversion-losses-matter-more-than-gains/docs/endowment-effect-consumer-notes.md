# Endowment Effect and Consumer Loss Framing Notes

## Endowment effect

The endowment effect occurs when ownership increases valuation. A person may require more to give up a good than they would have paid to acquire it.

## Consumer loss-framing channels

- free trials
- subscription renewals
- cancellation flows
- reward points
- loyalty tiers
- expiring discounts
- scarcity cues
- "you will lose access" warnings
- downgrade warnings
- saved settings and data
- switching costs

## Consumer-protection questions

- Is the loss warning accurate?
- Is scarcity real or artificial?
- Is cancellation easy?
- Is total cost visible?
- Are reference prices truthful?
- Does the interface inform or pressure?
- Does the firm benefit from confusion or inertia?

Loss aversion should not be used as a design license for dark patterns.
