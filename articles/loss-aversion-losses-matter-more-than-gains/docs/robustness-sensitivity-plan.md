# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate frame effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare gain-frame, loss-frame, and mixed-gamble outcomes separately.
3. Estimate heterogeneity by loss-aversion quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneity by income-security quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneity by numeracy quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneity by prior-loss exposure.
7. Estimate heterogeneity by trust quartile.
8. Vary loss-aversion coefficient ranges.
9. Vary gain and loss curvature parameters.
10. Vary reference points.
11. Compare mixed-gamble acceptance under different gain/loss ratios.
12. Test disposition-effect sale behavior under alternative price paths.
13. Test endowment-effect size under different ownership and market-price assumptions.
14. Compare behavioral effects and welfare effects separately.

## External validity

Real-world loss aversion depends on reference points, wealth, liquidity, trust, institutional history, material vulnerability, ownership, identity, bargaining power, and whether the loss is genuine, perceived, or strategically framed.
