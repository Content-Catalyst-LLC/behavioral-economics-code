# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include behavioral-economics teaching, household-finance research, policy-evaluation scaffolding, welfare analysis, institutional design, public-program evaluation, and synthetic simulation.

Inappropriate uses include punitive design, exploitative withdrawal penalties, manipulative subscriptions, coercive workplace productivity systems, welfare-reducing lock-ins, dark-pattern cancellation barriers, or using behavioral economics to shift structural economic problems onto individuals.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as financial advice, health advice, legal advice, product recommendations, or empirical claims about real households.
