# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare savings and welfare outcomes separately.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by present-bias quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by sophistication quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by liquidity-need quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by emergency-risk quartile.
7. Vary commitment-cost assumptions.
8. Vary automation-strength assumptions.
9. Vary flexibility assumptions.
10. Vary income-volatility assumptions.
11. Compare voluntary commitment, default commitment, and imposed commitment.
12. Evaluate whether penalties produce hardship.
13. Test whether stronger commitment dominates moderate commitment in welfare terms.

## External validity

Real-world commitment effects depend on income stability, liquidity needs, institutional trust, product design, digital access, financial literacy, emergency risk, household composition, debt, health, employment conditions, and the ability to opt out.
