# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without liquidity, leverage, and social-media controls.
2. Compare price-deviation and volatility outcomes across herding regimes.
3. Estimate shock-window drawdowns before and after negative news.
4. Vary herd-weight assumptions.
5. Vary private-signal quality.
6. Vary liquidity-depth assumptions.
7. Vary leverage-pressure assumptions.
8. Vary social-media intensity assumptions.
9. Vary loss-aversion parameters.
10. Vary reputation-pressure parameters.
11. Compare results with and without forced-selling feedback.
12. Distinguish common information from imitation in interpretation.

## External validity

Real-world herding depends on market structure, order-flow visibility, investor type, leverage, regulation, platform design, benchmark constraints, social media, liquidity provision, macro news, and institutional mandates.
