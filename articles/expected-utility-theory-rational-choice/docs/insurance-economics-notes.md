# Insurance Economics Notes

Expected utility explains why risk-averse households may buy insurance even when premiums exceed actuarially expected losses.

## Key concepts

- Expected loss.
- Premium loading.
- Deductibles.
- Coverage limits.
- Catastrophic downside risk.
- Risk premium.
- Certainty equivalent.
- Moral hazard.
- Adverse selection.
- Affordability constraints.
- Trust and product comprehension.

## Behavioral and institutional cautions

- Non-purchase may reflect liquidity constraints, not low risk aversion.
- People may overinsure salient small risks and underinsure catastrophic hidden risks.
- Insurance products may be confusing or distrusted.
- Disaster insurance demand may rise after salient events and fall as memory fades.
- Policy design should distinguish willingness to pay from ability to pay.
