# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include economic teaching, decision-theory demonstrations, synthetic insurance-demand modeling, portfolio-choice education, public-policy risk analysis, sustainability scenario analysis, welfare-methods scaffolding, and reproducible research workflows.

Inappropriate uses include individualized investment advice, real insurance pricing, real lending or underwriting, employee screening, benefit eligibility automation, manipulative financial-product design, or treating expected-utility outputs as morally complete welfare judgments.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as legal advice, financial advice, investment advice, insurance advice, actuarial advice, public-health advice, or empirical claims about real households, investors, insurers, public agencies, communities, or institutions.
