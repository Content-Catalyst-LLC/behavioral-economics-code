# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate outcomes with and without covariates.
2. Compare formal expected-utility choice and observed choice with frictions.
3. Estimate heterogeneity by risk-aversion quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneity by wealth quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneity by numeracy quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneity by liquidity-constraint quartile.
7. Estimate heterogeneity by trust quartile.
8. Vary CRRA parameter ranges.
9. Compare CRRA and CARA utility.
10. Vary insurance loading factors.
11. Vary probability of catastrophic loss.
12. Vary discount factors in sustainability policy examples.
13. Test whether conclusions are sensitive to utility curvature.
14. Compare expected-utility predictions with prospect-theory-style probability weighting overlays.

## External validity

Real-world expected-utility estimation depends on wealth, liquidity, trust, access, information, institutional context, ambiguity, reference points, probability perception, social norms, and distributional vulnerability.
