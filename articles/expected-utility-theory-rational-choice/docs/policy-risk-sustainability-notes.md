# Policy Risk and Sustainability Notes

Expected utility is important for policy decisions involving uncertain futures, catastrophic downside risk, and intergenerational welfare.

## Policy applications

- Climate mitigation.
- Climate adaptation.
- Infrastructure resilience.
- Disaster preparedness.
- Public health prevention.
- Financial regulation.
- Energy transition.
- Biodiversity protection.
- Water and food security.
- Technological risk.

## Key challenges

- Low-probability high-damage events.
- Deep uncertainty rather than known risk.
- Discounting future welfare.
- Unequal vulnerability.
- Irreversibility.
- Ecological non-substitutability.
- Future generations without market voice.
- Model uncertainty.
- Trust and institutional legitimacy.

Expected utility can clarify tradeoffs, but it should be paired with scenario analysis, robustness, precautionary reasoning, justice, and democratic accountability.
