# Welfare Analysis Notes

Behavioral regulation should not be evaluated only by compliance. Compliance is important, but a policy can increase compliance while imposing high administrative burden, enforcement costs, fear, distrust, or unequal access.

## Welfare components

The synthetic workflows define total welfare as a function of:

- social benefit from compliance
- utility of compliant action
- compliance cost
- administrative cost
- enforcement cost
- burden cost
- distributional incidence

## Welfare accounting

A stylized welfare expression is:

```text
W = compliance_utility + social_benefit - compliance_cost - administrative_cost - enforcement_cost
```

## Distributional welfare

The scaffold reports welfare by:

- trust quartile
- burden-sensitivity quartile
- compliance-capacity quartile
- private-gain-from-noncompliance quartile

## Policy relevance

This scaffold supports:

- tax-compliance policy evaluation
- consumer-protection compliance analysis
- public-benefit administration research
- digital-consent regulation
- environmental permitting and reporting systems
- financial disclosure and compliance design
- administrative burden and access research
