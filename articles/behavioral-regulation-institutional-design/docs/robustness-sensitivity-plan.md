# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Professional regulatory policy workflows should include robustness checks.

## Recommended checks

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare OLS, logit/probit for binary compliance, and OLS welfare models.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by trust quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by burden-sensitivity quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by compliance-capacity quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by private-gain-from-noncompliance quartile.
7. Test alternative social-benefit weights.
8. Test alternative administrative-cost assumptions.
9. Test alternative enforcement-cost assumptions.
10. Compare compliance effects with welfare effects.
11. Report standard errors and confidence intervals where packages are available.

## Placebo checks for real data

- Pre-treatment compliance.
- Baseline filing or reporting behavior.
- Baseline institutional trust.
- Prior enforcement history.
- Pre-policy trend in error rates, late filings, complaints, or applications.

## External validity

Real regulatory effects depend on institutional capacity, legal domain, regulated population, compliance technology, enforcement credibility, agency legitimacy, and political context. Synthetic results should not be interpreted as real-world estimates.
