# Welfare and Autonomy Notes

Status quo bias interventions should be evaluated by welfare, autonomy, transparency, and distribution, not adoption alone.

A stylized welfare expression is:

```text
Welfare = Objective Value + Autonomy + Reliability - Switching Cost - Administrative Burden - Hardship
```

## Core welfare questions

- Does the default support a goal the affected people are likely to endorse?
- Does the default preserve meaningful exit?
- Does switching support reduce real burden or merely push users toward an institutional preference?
- Does active choice improve autonomy or create decision fatigue?
- Who benefits from inertia?
- Who bears switching costs?
- Is the current arrangement legitimate, merely familiar, or actively exploitative?
- Are vulnerable groups harmed by defaults, opt-outs, or administrative burden?

## Recommended diagnostics

- Adoption rate by regime.
- Welfare by regime.
- Effective switching cost.
- Effective status quo premium.
- Effective perceived loss.
- Treatment effects by switching-cost quartile.
- Treatment effects by loss-aversion quartile.
- Treatment effects by decision-fatigue quartile.
- Sensitivity to default shift, disclosure quality, and switching support.
