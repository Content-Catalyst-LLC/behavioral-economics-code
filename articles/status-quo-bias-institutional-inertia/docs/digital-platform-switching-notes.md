# Digital Platform Switching Notes

Digital platforms can intensify status quo bias by making staying automatic and switching effortful.

## Inertia-amplifying features

- Automatic subscription renewal.
- Cancellation friction.
- Opaque billing.
- Bundled services.
- Loss of saved preferences.
- Data lock-in.
- Social-network lock-in.
- Proprietary file formats.
- App ecosystem lock-in.
- Hidden switching costs.
- Renewal notices that are unclear or poorly timed.

## User-aligned countermeasures

- Clear renewal notices.
- Easy cancellation.
- Data portability.
- Interoperability.
- Subscription dashboards.
- Transparent price changes.
- Switching-cost disclosure.
- Account export tools.
- Privacy settings that are visible and revisable.
- Defaults that are reviewed periodically.

The goal is not to eliminate convenience. It is to prevent convenience from becoming captivity.
