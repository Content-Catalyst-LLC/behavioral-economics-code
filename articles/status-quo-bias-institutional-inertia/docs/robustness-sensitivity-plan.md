# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare adoption and welfare outcomes separately.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by switching-cost quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by loss-aversion quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by decision-fatigue quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by uncertainty-sensitivity quartile.
7. Estimate heterogeneous effects by sophistication quartile.
8. Vary default-shift assumptions.
9. Vary switching-support assumptions.
10. Vary disclosure-quality assumptions.
11. Vary status quo premium assumptions.
12. Vary perceived-loss assumptions.
13. Compare active choice versus pro-switching defaults in welfare terms.
14. Test whether high-switching-cost agents benefit or are burdened.

## External validity

Real-world status quo bias effects depend on market structure, search costs, administrative complexity, trust, digital access, literacy, switching friction, data portability, income stability, regulation, institutional power, and the legitimacy of the default.
