# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include behavioral-economics teaching, consumer-protection research, default-policy evaluation, public-program design, digital-platform governance review, sustainability-transition analysis, institutional reform analysis, and synthetic simulation.

Inappropriate uses include dark-pattern design, subscription trap design, anti-competitive switching friction, exploitative defaults, administrative exclusion, misleading opt-outs, data lock-in, regulatory capture, or using behavioral insights to preserve harmful status quos.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as legal advice, financial advice, product recommendations, empirical claims about real consumers, or operational guidance for manipulating users.
