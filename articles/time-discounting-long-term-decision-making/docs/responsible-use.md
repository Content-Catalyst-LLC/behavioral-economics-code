# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include behavioral-economics teaching, intertemporal-choice research, policy-evaluation scaffolding, household-finance analysis, climate and sustainability governance analysis, digital-platform governance review, and synthetic simulation.

Inappropriate uses include financial advice, investment advice, credit product marketing, manipulative subscription design, engagement-maximization dark patterns, productivity surveillance, welfare-reducing lock-ins, predatory credit, punitive behavioral governance, or using discounting language to dismiss structural constraints.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as financial advice, health advice, legal advice, product recommendations, investment analysis, or empirical claims about real households, workers, students, patients, users, firms, governments, or public institutions.
