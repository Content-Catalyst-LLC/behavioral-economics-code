# Digital Platform Time Architecture Notes

Digital platforms shape time discounting by deciding how quickly users can act, how visible future costs are, and how immediate rewards are delivered.

## Immediacy-amplifying features

- One-click purchase.
- Buy-now-pay-later checkout.
- Subscription auto-renewal.
- Instant trading.
- Infinite scroll.
- Autoplay.
- Push notifications.
- Streaks and gamified urgency.
- Algorithmic novelty.
- Variable rewards.
- Hidden cumulative cost.

## User-aligned countermeasures

- Cooling-off periods for high-risk actions.
- Subscription dashboards and renewal reminders.
- Cumulative cost displays.
- Debt repayment visibility.
- Friction around speculative or high-cost financial actions.
- Default savings and payment reminders.
- Commitment settings under user control.
- Timely warnings before irreversible decisions.

The ethical question is whether the platform compresses time to serve the user’s goals or to exploit behavioral vulnerability.
