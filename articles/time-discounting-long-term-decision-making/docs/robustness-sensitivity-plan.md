# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare delayed-choice outcomes and welfare outcomes separately.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by present-bias quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by discount-factor quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by sophistication quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by liquidity-need quartile.
7. Estimate heterogeneous effects by future-goal-value quartile.
8. Vary discount-factor assumptions.
9. Vary present-bias assumptions.
10. Vary commitment-support assumptions.
11. Vary flexibility assumptions.
12. Vary present-value discount-rate assumptions for long-horizon public goods.
13. Compare welfare implications under alternate climate/infrastructure discount rates.
14. Test whether commitment support improves delayed choice without excessive flexibility loss.

## External validity

Real-world discounting effects depend on income stability, debt burden, inflation expectations, health, stress, institutional trust, platform design, financial literacy, public benefits, work schedules, household composition, ecological risk, and the ability to opt out.
