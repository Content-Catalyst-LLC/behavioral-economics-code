# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include behavioral-economics teaching, framing research, consumer-protection evaluation, health-risk communication, sustainability communication, digital-interface governance, public-policy messaging analysis, and synthetic simulation.

Inappropriate uses include manipulative advertising, deceptive price framing, hidden-fee design, dark-pattern interfaces, fear-based coercion, greenwashing, misleading risk communication, or using framing to bypass informed judgment.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, product recommendations, or empirical claims about real consumers, patients, platform users, voters, or public audiences.
