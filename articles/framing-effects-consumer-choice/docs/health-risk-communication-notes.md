# Health and Risk Communication Notes

Health decisions are strongly shaped by how risks are presented.

## Common framing contrasts

- Survival rate versus mortality rate.
- Relative risk reduction versus absolute risk reduction.
- Percentages versus natural frequencies.
- Individual benefit versus community benefit.
- Early detection versus overdiagnosis risk.
- Treatment benefit versus side-effect burden.
- Certainty versus uncertainty.

## Responsible health framing

- Present absolute risk when possible.
- Present relative risk with baseline risk.
- Use natural frequencies for comprehension.
- Avoid fear-based exaggeration.
- Include uncertainty honestly.
- Use plain language.
- Support shared decision-making.
- Test comprehension across literacy and numeracy groups.

Health framing is part of informed consent. It should clarify rather than steer without understanding.
