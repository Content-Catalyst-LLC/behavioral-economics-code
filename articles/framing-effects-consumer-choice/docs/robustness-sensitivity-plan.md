# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare risky-choice outcomes and comprehension outcomes separately.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by loss-aversion quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by numeracy quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by trust quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by decision-fatigue quartile.
7. Vary frame-strength assumptions.
8. Vary salience assumptions.
9. Vary disclosure-quality assumptions.
10. Compare gain/loss frames with balanced absolute-risk framing.
11. Test whether frames change behavior without improving comprehension.
12. Test whether high-numeracy and low-numeracy groups respond differently.
13. Evaluate welfare proxy under alternate weights for comprehension and manipulation risk.
14. Compare behavior change, decision quality, and autonomy.

## External validity

Real-world framing effects depend on trust, literacy, numeracy, prior beliefs, stakes, product quality, institutional credibility, cultural context, language, visual design, legal requirements, and whether the frame changes information rather than presentation.
