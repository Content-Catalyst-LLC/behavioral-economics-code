# Welfare and Comprehension Notes

Framing interventions should be evaluated by comprehension, autonomy, decision quality, and welfare, not behavior change alone.

A stylized welfare expression is:

```text
Welfare = Decision Quality + Comprehension + Autonomy - Manipulation Risk - Behavioral Burden
```

## Core welfare questions

- Does the frame improve understanding?
- Does it make total cost, absolute risk, or long-term consequences clearer?
- Does it shift behavior without improving comprehension?
- Does it obscure tradeoffs or make them visible?
- Does the frame serve the user, the seller, the platform, or the public?
- Who is most affected by the frame?
- Does the frame exploit fear, urgency, scarcity, identity, or limited attention?
- Is the frame transparent and publicly defensible?

## Recommended diagnostics

- Choice rate by frame.
- Comprehension by frame.
- Welfare proxy by frame.
- Risky-choice rate by loss-aversion quartile.
- Comprehension by numeracy quartile.
- Heterogeneity by trust and fatigue.
- Sensitivity to frame strength, salience, and disclosure quality.
