# Sustainability Framing Notes

Sustainability framing shapes whether environmental action is understood as sacrifice, innovation, stewardship, justice, health, resilience, risk reduction, or cost.

## Useful but incomplete frames

- Avoided climate damage.
- Public-health protection.
- Household savings.
- Energy independence.
- Clean technology innovation.
- Intergenerational responsibility.
- Environmental justice.
- Local resilience.
- Stewardship.
- Waste reduction.

## Cautions

- Catastrophe-only frames may produce fatigue.
- Opportunity-only frames may hide real transition costs.
- Green labels may become greenwashing.
- Consumer-responsibility frames can obscure institutional responsibility.
- National-competitiveness frames can obscure global justice.
- Technical-efficiency frames can obscure distributional burdens.

Responsible sustainability framing should make urgency, agency, tradeoffs, evidence, and justice visible together.
