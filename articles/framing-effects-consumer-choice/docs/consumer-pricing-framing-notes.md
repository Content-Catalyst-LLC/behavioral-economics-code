# Consumer Pricing and Framing Notes

Consumer prices are rarely interpreted in isolation. They are framed through anchors, discounts, bundles, monthly payments, subscription terms, and checkout flows.

## Framing mechanisms

- Reference prices.
- Was/now price displays.
- Percent discounts.
- Monthly versus annual cost.
- Total repayment versus installment payment.
- Subscription auto-renewal.
- Free trials that convert to paid plans.
- Product bundles.
- Limited-time urgency.
- Popular-plan badges.
- Low monthly payment emphasis.
- Hidden fees and add-ons.

## Consumer-aligned countermeasures

- Total-cost displays.
- Unit pricing.
- Annual subscription summaries.
- Clear cancellation rights.
- Renewal reminders.
- Absolute cost comparisons.
- Transparent fees.
- Plain-language financing terms.
- Side-by-side comparable information.
- Warnings when promotional pricing expires.

The goal is not to remove persuasion from markets. It is to prevent price presentation from defeating informed choice.
