# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include behavioral-economics teaching, cognitive-bias research, consumer-protection analysis, behavioral policy evaluation, risk-communication design, sustainability governance, platform accountability, institutional decision review, and synthetic simulation.

Inappropriate uses include manipulative nudging, dark-pattern design, exploitative pricing, fear-based persuasion, deceptive risk communication, gamified speculation, engagement-maximizing bias amplification, or using behavioral insights to bypass informed judgment.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, investment advice, product recommendations, or empirical claims about real consumers, households, investors, platform users, public agencies, communities, or institutions.
