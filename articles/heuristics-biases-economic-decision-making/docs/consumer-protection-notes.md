# Consumer Protection Notes

Consumer markets often exploit predictable heuristics.

## Common exploitation patterns

- Inflated reference prices.
- Fake urgency.
- Scarcity cues.
- Hidden fees.
- Low monthly-payment anchors.
- Default subscriptions.
- Difficult cancellation.
- Gamified trading.
- Manipulated reviews.
- Misleading comparison tables.
- Social-proof badges.
- Overly salient rewards.
- Under-disclosed total cost.

## Consumer-aligned interventions

- Total-cost disclosure.
- Honest reference pricing.
- Easy cancellation.
- Base-rate and comparison information.
- Clear risk warnings.
- Cooling-off periods for high-risk decisions.
- Review-integrity rules.
- Plain-language disclosures.
- Standardized comparison metrics.
- Dark-pattern audits.

Behavioral evidence should protect real consumers, not train firms to exploit predictable error.
