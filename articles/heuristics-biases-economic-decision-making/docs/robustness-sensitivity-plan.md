# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare signed judgment error and absolute error separately.
3. Compare decision quality and welfare proxy separately.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by correction-capacity quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by numeracy quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by domain-knowledge quartile.
7. Estimate heterogeneous effects by cognitive-load quartile.
8. Estimate heterogeneous effects by confidence quartile.
9. Vary heuristic signal scale.
10. Vary disclosure-quality assumptions.
11. Vary debiasing-support assumptions.
12. Vary welfare weights on accuracy, comprehension, autonomy, burden, and manipulation risk.
13. Test whether interventions change behavior without improving judgment quality.
14. Test whether high confidence amplifies welfare loss when judgment error is high.

## External validity

Real-world heuristic effects depend on feedback quality, institutional trust, cognitive load, income security, prior experience, domain expertise, local risk, media exposure, platform design, and structural constraints.
