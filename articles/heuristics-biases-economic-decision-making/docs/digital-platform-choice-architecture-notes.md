# Digital Platform Choice Architecture Notes

Digital platforms structure judgment through interface design, ranking, default settings, algorithmic estimates, and attention systems.

## Platform bias channels

- Recommendation feeds.
- Trending lists.
- Rankings.
- Defaults.
- Progress bars.
- Push notifications.
- Suggested prices.
- Suggested tips.
- Risk scores.
- Estimated values.
- Popular badges.
- Review sorting.
- Autoplay.
- Checkout sequencing.
- Friction asymmetry.

## Governance questions

- What heuristic does the interface activate?
- Does the design increase comprehension or merely conversion?
- Does it hide total cost or long-term consequences?
- Does it amplify fear, urgency, overconfidence, or inertia?
- Are algorithmic estimates auditable?
- Do users have meaningful alternatives and exit?
- Are distributional harms monitored?

A platform decision environment should be evaluated by decision quality, autonomy, welfare, and contestability, not only engagement.
