# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare OLS with logit/probit for binary rejection or support outcomes.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by alpha quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by beta quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by merit-belief quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by redistribution-norm quartile.
7. Estimate heterogeneous effects by institutional-trust quartile.
8. Vary alpha and beta weights.
9. Vary process-legitimacy assumptions.
10. Vary tax-rate and transfer assumptions.
11. Compare material-payoff rankings with social-preference welfare rankings.
12. Report uncertainty where packages are available.

## External validity

Real-world inequality aversion depends on culture, income position, labor-market structure, tax salience, institutional trust, political framing, race/class/gender relations, beliefs about merit, and the perceived legitimacy of the distribution-generating process.
