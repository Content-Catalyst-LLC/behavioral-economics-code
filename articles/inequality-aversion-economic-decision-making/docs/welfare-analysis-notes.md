# Welfare Analysis Notes

Inequality aversion should not be reduced to a generic dislike of unequal outcomes. People evaluate distributions in relation to process, merit, reciprocity, legitimacy, power, and opportunity.

A stylized welfare expression is:

```text
W = material_output - inequality_penalty + process_legitimacy + social_preference_utility
```

## Core welfare questions

- Does the distributional regime improve material payoff but reduce social-preference utility?
- Does redistribution increase utility among high-alpha agents while reducing utility among high-merit-belief agents?
- Does process legitimacy moderate inequality aversion?
- Does acceptance of inequality reflect genuine legitimacy or constrained choice?
- Does an intervention reduce inequality without reducing broad material sufficiency?

## Recommended diagnostics

- Material payoff ranking versus social-preference utility ranking.
- Average treatment effects by alpha quartile and beta quartile.
- Redistribution support by income group and merit belief.
- Robustness to alternative inequality penalties.
- Distinction between advantageous and disadvantageous inequality aversion.
