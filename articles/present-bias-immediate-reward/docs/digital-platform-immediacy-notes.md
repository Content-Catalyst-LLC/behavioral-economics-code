# Digital Platform Immediacy Notes

Digital platforms often amplify present bias by reducing the time between impulse and action.

## Immediacy-amplifying features

- Infinite scroll.
- Autoplay.
- Notifications.
- Likes and rapid social feedback.
- One-click purchase.
- Frictionless checkout.
- Instant trading.
- Buy-now-pay-later interfaces.
- Variable rewards.
- Streaks and gamified pressure.
- Subscription renewal by inertia.
- Hidden cumulative cost.

## User-aligned countermeasures

- Cooling-off periods for high-risk actions.
- Spending and trading friction.
- Default reminders for long-term goals.
- Clear cumulative cost displays.
- Subscription dashboards.
- User-controlled app limits.
- Commitment settings.
- High-risk product warnings before action.
- Scheduled focus modes.
- Transparent opt-outs.

The ethical question is not whether design influences behavior. It does. The question is whose welfare the design serves.
