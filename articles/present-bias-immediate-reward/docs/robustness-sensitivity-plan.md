# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare delayed-choice rates and welfare separately.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by present-bias quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by sophistication quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by liquidity-need quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by temptation-strength quartile.
7. Vary commitment-cost assumptions.
8. Vary reminder-strength assumptions.
9. Vary flexibility assumptions.
10. Vary future-goal value assumptions.
11. Compare weak, medium, and strong commitment in welfare terms.
12. Test whether strong commitment creates hardship for high-liquidity-need agents.
13. Compare present-bias intervention effects under alternate welfare weights.

## External validity

Real-world present-bias effects depend on income stability, debt burden, health status, stress, platform design, financial literacy, institutional trust, public benefits, work schedules, household composition, and the ability to opt out.
