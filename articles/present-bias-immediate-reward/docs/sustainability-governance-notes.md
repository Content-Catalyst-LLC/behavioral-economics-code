# Sustainability and Long-Horizon Governance Notes

Present bias matters for sustainability because environmental and infrastructure systems often require immediate cost for delayed benefit.

## Present-bias sustainability channels

- Immediate costs of climate mitigation are politically salient.
- Future harms are discounted or abstract.
- Benefits are delayed and distributed.
- Political cycles reward near-term visible wins.
- Deferred maintenance avoids present budget pain while increasing future risk.
- Slow ecological degradation lacks immediate feedback.
- Future generations cannot vote in present decisions.

## Institutional responses

- Long-term public investment rules.
- Climate targets with measurable interim checkpoints.
- Infrastructure maintenance funds.
- Independent fiscal and climate oversight.
- Default efficiency standards.
- Conservation commitments.
- Resilience-budgeting requirements.
- Public dashboards that make future risk visible.

Sustainability governance requires protecting long-term public goods without imposing unjust immediate burdens on people already facing scarcity.
