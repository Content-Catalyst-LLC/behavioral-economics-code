# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include behavioral-economics teaching, intertemporal-choice research, policy-evaluation scaffolding, household-finance analysis, digital-platform governance review, sustainability-governance analysis, and synthetic simulation.

Inappropriate uses include punitive design, exploitative lock-ins, manipulative subscription systems, engagement-maximization dark patterns, productivity surveillance, welfare-reducing defaults, predatory credit, speculative trading nudges, or shifting structural social problems onto individuals through behavioral framing.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as financial advice, health advice, legal advice, product recommendations, or empirical claims about real households, workers, students, patients, users, or institutions.
