# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include behavioral-finance teaching, investor-protection research, synthetic simulation, market-stability analysis, policy-evaluation scaffolding, and reproducible methods demonstration.

Inappropriate uses include investment advice, trading advice, market prediction, coordinated trading, speculative promotion, retail-investor targeting, encouragement of frequent trading, leverage promotion, product marketing, or exploitation of behavioral bias.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as forecasts, trading signals, recommendations, or claims about any specific asset, brokerage, platform, or investor population.
