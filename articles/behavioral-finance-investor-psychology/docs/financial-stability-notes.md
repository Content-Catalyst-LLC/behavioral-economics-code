# Financial-Stability Notes

Behavioral finance matters for financial stability when individual bias scales into market-level dynamics.

## Behavioral-stability channels

- Overconfidence can increase turnover and disagreement-driven trading.
- Herding can amplify bubbles and crashes.
- Loss aversion can delay selling and then intensify capitulation.
- Anchoring can slow belief updating.
- Platform salience can make crowd signals more visible.
- Low trading friction can increase reaction speed.
- Leverage and liquidity constraints can convert behavioral pressure into systemic stress.

## Suggested metrics

- Absolute mispricing.
- Buy-rate synchronization.
- Mean trade intensity.
- Trading-cost drag.
- Volatility proxy.
- Peak-to-trough drawdown.
- Herd-signal intensity.
- Platform-salience sensitivity.
- Liquidity-adjusted price impact.
