# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without friction and platform-salience controls.
2. Compare mean mispricing and absolute mispricing across regimes.
3. Compare trading intensity and cost drag across regimes.
4. Vary behavioral-distortion scale.
5. Vary trading-friction assumptions.
6. Vary platform-salience assumptions.
7. Vary overconfidence, loss-aversion, anchoring, and herd-weight distributions.
8. Estimate sensitivity to alternative fundamental-value processes.
9. Compare results with and without investor heterogeneity.
10. Compare results with and without prospect-theory loss asymmetry.
11. Evaluate household/investor-protection outcomes separately from market-level outcomes.
12. Distinguish market participation from investor welfare.

## External validity

Real-world behavioral finance depends on market regime, investor composition, product access, financial literacy, income, wealth, liquidity needs, platform design, institutional incentives, regulation, and macroeconomic context.
