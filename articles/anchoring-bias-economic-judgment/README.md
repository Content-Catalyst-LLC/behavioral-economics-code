# Anchoring Bias in Economic Judgment

This article-level folder supports the article **Anchoring Bias in Economic Judgment**.

Article URL: https://sustainablecatalyst.com/anchoring-bias-economic-judgment/  
Repository folder: https://github.com/Content-Catalyst-LLC/behavioral-economics-code/tree/main/articles/anchoring-bias-economic-judgment

## Purpose

This is a professional economist-facing computational scaffold for studying anchoring bias, economic judgment, incomplete adjustment, reference prices, negotiation anchors, wage bargaining, housing appraisals, asset valuation, inflation expectations, policy benchmarks, carbon baselines, digital-platform anchors, algorithmic estimates, and institutional decision architecture.

The scaffold is designed for behavioral economics, applied microeconomics, behavioral finance, labor economics, housing economics, consumer protection, public economics, digital-platform governance, climate economics, sustainability policy, institutional economics, and reproducible teaching examples.

## Research questions

1. How do low anchors, high anchors, neutral anchors, and counter-anchor context affect final estimates?
2. How does incomplete adjustment create biased estimates under uncertainty?
3. How do numeracy, confidence, domain knowledge, and cognitive load moderate anchor effects?
4. When does an anchor improve judgment, and when does it distort judgment?
5. How do list prices, reference prices, opening offers, salary bands, appraisal values, and policy baselines shape economic outcomes?
6. How do algorithmic estimates and digital-interface benchmarks become anchors?
7. How do outdated sustainability baselines and historical benchmarks affect climate and infrastructure decisions?
8. How should benchmark design be evaluated for welfare, fairness, autonomy, and distributional consequences?
9. What robustness checks are required before treating an anchoring intervention as welfare-improving?

## Econometric and computational design

The scaffold includes synthetic anchoring-regime data that support:

- high-anchor and low-anchor simulations
- neutral-anchor comparison regimes
- high-anchor-with-counter-context workflows
- incomplete-adjustment models
- reference-price diagnostics
- negotiation-anchor examples
- wage-bargaining and salary-band scaffolds
- housing-appraisal examples
- consumer-price and monthly-payment notes
- digital-platform and algorithmic-anchor notes
- policy-benchmark and sustainability-baseline notes
- treatment-effect estimation
- heterogeneous treatment effects by adjustment rate, numeracy, domain knowledge, cognitive load, and confidence
- decision-quality and welfare proxy analysis
- robustness and sensitivity checks
- Stata, R, Python, Julia, SQL, and systems-language examples

## Responsible use

This repository is for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, policy-evaluation scaffolding, consumer-protection analysis, labor-market transparency, housing and appraisal research, benchmark-design review, sustainability-baseline analysis, digital-platform governance, public-interest institutional learning, and reproducible behavioral-economics workflows. It is not a tool for manipulative pricing, misleading reference prices, exploitative negotiation, low-ball salary anchoring, opaque algorithmic estimates, deceptive monthly-payment framing, or using behavioral insights to bypass informed judgment.
