# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare estimate bias and absolute error separately.
3. Compare decision-quality and welfare-proxy outcomes separately.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by effective-adjustment quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by numeracy quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by domain-knowledge quartile.
7. Estimate heterogeneous effects by cognitive-load quartile.
8. Estimate heterogeneous effects by confidence quartile.
9. Vary anchor distance from the true value.
10. Vary disclosure-quality assumptions.
11. Vary counter-anchor-support assumptions.
12. Vary welfare weights on accuracy, autonomy, confidence, and cognitive burden.
13. Test whether counter-context reduces high-anchor bias.
14. Test whether strong confidence amplifies error among biased estimates.

## External validity

Real-world anchor effects depend on uncertainty, information quality, bargaining power, credibility of the anchor, domain expertise, cognitive load, time pressure, institutional authority, and whether the anchor contains genuine information.
