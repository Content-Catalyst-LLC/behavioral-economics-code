# Digital Platform and Algorithmic Anchor Notes

Digital interfaces create anchors through generated estimates, recommended prices, ranked options, comparison tiers, and default numbers.

## Platform anchors

- Recommended price.
- Suggested bid.
- Typical price.
- Estimated value.
- Fair market estimate.
- Risk score.
- Credit limit.
- Popular plan.
- Default tip.
- Suggested donation.
- Recommended contribution.
- Appraisal estimate.
- Dynamic price.
- Projected return.
- Algorithmic wage estimate.

## Governance questions

- Is the anchor labeled as estimate, advertisement, model output, or recommendation?
- What uncertainty is disclosed?
- Is the model auditable?
- Does personalization create unequal anchors?
- Does the platform benefit when the user accepts the anchor?
- Can the user contest or override the anchor?
- Are outcomes monitored for distributional harm?

Algorithmic anchors should not gain authority merely because they look technical.
