# Housing and Appraisal Anchoring Notes

Housing valuation depends on anchors such as listing prices, appraisals, purchase prices, comparable sales, mortgage preapproval amounts, and neighborhood expectations.

## Key concerns

- Listing prices can shape buyer and appraiser expectations.
- Purchase prices can anchor sellers against market adjustment.
- Comparable sales can reproduce historical segregation and disinvestment.
- Appraisals can appear objective while embedding prior valuation patterns.
- Mortgage preapproval can anchor buyers to maximum debt capacity rather than prudent affordability.
- Algorithmic valuation models can obscure uncertainty and bias.

## Research questions

- Do listing prices affect final sale prices after controlling for property fundamentals?
- Do appraisals differ by neighborhood composition after comparable controls?
- Do preapproval amounts alter purchase-price distributions?
- Do algorithmic valuations reduce or reproduce historical appraisal bias?
