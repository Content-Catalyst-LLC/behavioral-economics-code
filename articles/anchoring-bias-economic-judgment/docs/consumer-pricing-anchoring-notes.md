# Consumer Pricing and Anchoring Notes

Pricing environments are saturated with anchors that shape perceived value and affordability.

## Anchoring channels

- Was/now prices.
- Manufacturer suggested retail prices.
- Percent discounts.
- Premium tier comparisons.
- Popular plan labels.
- Monthly payments.
- Minimum payments.
- Promotional rates.
- Credit limits.
- Preapproved borrowing amounts.
- Suggested tips.
- Suggested donations.
- Buy-now-pay-later installments.
- Subscription tiers.

## Consumer-aligned practices

- Show total cost.
- Use truthful reference prices.
- Disclose promotional expiration.
- Display comparable unit prices.
- Separate affordability from approval.
- Avoid misleading discount anchors.
- Provide annualized subscription cost.
- Make fees visible.
- Explain uncertainty around algorithmic price estimates.

A price anchor should clarify value, not manufacture the illusion of savings.
