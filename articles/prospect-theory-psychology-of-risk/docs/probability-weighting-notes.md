# Probability Weighting Notes

Prospect theory replaces linear probabilities with decision weights.

## One-parameter weighting function

```text
pi(p) = p^gamma / (p^gamma + (1-p)^gamma)^(1/gamma)
```

## Interpretation

- Small probabilities may be overweighted.
- Moderate and high probabilities may be underweighted.
- Certainty can have special psychological force.
- Rare gains may drive lottery behavior.
- Rare losses may drive insurance demand or catastrophic-risk salience.

## Caution

Probability weighting should be distinguished from:

- misinformation
- ambiguity aversion
- distrust
- salience
- availability bias
- lived exposure to real hazards
- uncertainty in official estimates
