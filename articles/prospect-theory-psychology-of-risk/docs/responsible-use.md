# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include behavioral-economics teaching, decision-theory demonstrations, behavioral-finance research, consumer-protection analysis, public-policy evaluation, risk-communication design, sustainability governance, digital-platform accountability, institutional decision review, and synthetic simulation.

Inappropriate uses include manipulative loss framing, dark-pattern cancellation design, deceptive scarcity messages, gambling-like engagement design, predatory trading-interface design, exploitative insurance messaging, fear-based policy communication, or using behavioral insights to bypass informed judgment.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as legal advice, financial advice, investment advice, insurance advice, public-health advice, policy advice, or empirical claims about real households, investors, insurers, firms, platforms, communities, public agencies, or institutions.
