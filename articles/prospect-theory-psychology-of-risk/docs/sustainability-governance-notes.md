# Sustainability Governance Notes

Sustainability decisions are shaped by prospect-theory dynamics because many environmental policies create immediate visible costs to prevent long-term, distributed, or uncertain losses.

## Common short-term perceived losses

- higher prices
- reduced convenience
- stranded assets
- job transitions
- changed routines
- regulatory compliance
- land-use changes
- infrastructure disruption

## Long-term losses from inaction

- climate damage
- flood risk
- heat exposure
- biodiversity decline
- water insecurity
- crop failure
- public-health harms
- displacement
- infrastructure failure
- intergenerational loss

## Responsible communication

- make hidden losses visible
- avoid manipulation and despair
- pair urgency with agency
- provide transition support
- compensate genuine losses
- do not preserve unjust privilege
- include affected communities
- evaluate distributional effects
