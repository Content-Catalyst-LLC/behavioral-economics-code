# Consumer, Platform, and Policy Notes

## Consumer choice

- reference prices
- discounts and surcharges
- fees
- shipping charges
- free trials
- subscriptions
- cancellation flows
- loyalty points
- scarcity cues
- downgrade warnings

## Digital platforms

- algorithmic estimates as reference points
- personalized scarcity
- gamified gains/losses
- streaks and badges
- trading-app salience
- engagement-maximizing probability distortion
- dark-pattern cancellation design

## Public policy

- taxes and rebates
- fines and incentives
- retirement defaults
- public-health risk communication
- disaster preparedness
- consumer protection
- climate mitigation and adaptation
- transition compensation

Behavioral design should support comprehension, agency, fairness, and long-term welfare rather than manipulating loss aversion or probability distortion.
