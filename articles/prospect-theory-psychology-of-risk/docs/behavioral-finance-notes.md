# Behavioral Finance Notes

Prospect theory is central to behavioral finance because investors often evaluate outcomes relative to purchase prices, prior highs, recent losses, account balances, and benchmarks.

## Relevant patterns

- disposition effect
- reluctance to realize losses
- selling winners too early
- break-even psychology
- excessive risk-taking after losses
- speculative demand for lottery-like assets
- volatility salience
- loss-framed dashboards
- benchmark-driven regret

## Research cautions

Investor behavior may also reflect tax strategy, liquidity needs, transaction costs, beliefs, private information, portfolio constraints, or financial advice.

A robust workflow should compare prospect-theory explanations with institutional and informational explanations.
