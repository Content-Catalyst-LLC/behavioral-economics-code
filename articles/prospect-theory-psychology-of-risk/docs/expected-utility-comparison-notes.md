# Expected Utility Comparison Notes

Expected utility evaluates final outcomes through utility and probability. Prospect theory evaluates gains and losses around reference points and transforms probabilities into decision weights.

## Expected utility

```text
EU = sum_s p_s u(x_s)
```

## Prospect theory

```text
PT = sum_s pi(p_s) v(x_s - r)
```

## Comparison questions

- Do PT and EU predict the same risky choice?
- Where do they disagree?
- Which parameters drive disagreement?
- Does disagreement arise from loss aversion, probability weighting, reference points, or utility curvature?
- Does disagreement matter for welfare or only for behavior?

## Real-world caution

Expected utility may serve as a normative benchmark, while prospect theory may serve as a descriptive model. Neither automatically resolves welfare, rights, distribution, legitimacy, or justice.
