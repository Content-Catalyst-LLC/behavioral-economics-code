# Fourfold Pattern of Risk Attitudes

Prospect theory helps explain four common risk-attitude patterns.

| Domain | Probability | Common Pattern | Example |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Gains | High | Risk aversion | Prefer sure gain over likely larger gain |
| Gains | Low | Risk seeking | Lottery tickets, speculative upside |
| Losses | High | Risk seeking | Gamble to avoid near-certain loss |
| Losses | Low | Risk aversion | Insurance against rare catastrophe |

## Mechanisms

- Loss aversion.
- Diminishing sensitivity.
- Probability weighting.
- Certainty effect.
- Reference dependence.

## Policy relevance

Risk communication should not assume that people respond uniformly to probability. The same statistical risk may be experienced differently depending on gain/loss frame, salience, trust, and reference point.
