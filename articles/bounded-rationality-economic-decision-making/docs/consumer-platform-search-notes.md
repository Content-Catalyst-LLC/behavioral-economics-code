# Consumer and Platform Search Notes

Bounded rationality matters in consumer markets because people face search costs, switching costs, hidden fees, contract complexity, privacy terms, rankings, recommendations, and digital interface design.

## Consumer search frictions

- too many options
- unclear comparison features
- hidden fees
- long contracts
- misleading reference prices
- complex plan terms
- renewal inertia
- cancellation friction
- switching costs

## Platform mechanisms

- default settings
- recommendation rankings
- friction asymmetry
- personalized prompts
- scarcity cues
- social proof
- engagement-optimized feeds
- algorithmic estimates as anchors

## Governance questions

- Is exit easy?
- Are fees visible?
- Are choices comparable?
- Are defaults welfare-supporting?
- Is personalization exploiting vulnerability?
- Is search simplified honestly or manipulatively?
