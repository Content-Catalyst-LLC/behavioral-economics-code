# Sustainability Decision Systems Notes

Sustainability decisions involve long time horizons, uncertainty, complex feedback systems, collective action, and shifting baselines.

## Bounded-rationality challenges

- delayed consequences
- diffuse causality
- probabilistic risk
- scientific complexity
- misinformation
- short-term cost salience
- feedback delay
- ecological shifting baselines
- collective-action problems

## System supports

- clear labels
- trusted intermediaries
- well-designed defaults
- rebates and automatic enrollment
- resilient infrastructure
- robust decision-making
- adaptive governance
- scenario planning
- feedback loops
- community participation
- just transition support

## Key principle

Sustainable decision systems should reduce cognitive burden while preserving agency, equity, transparency, and democratic accountability.
