# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include behavioral-economics teaching, search-cost modeling, administrative-burden analysis, policy-simplification design, consumer-protection analysis, organizational-learning workflows, sustainability governance, digital-platform accountability, institutional decision review, and synthetic simulation.

Inappropriate uses include designing confusing disclosures, hidden fees, burdensome public programs, manipulative defaults, dark-pattern interfaces, friction asymmetry, exclusionary administrative systems, predatory financial products, or complexity that exploits cognitive limits.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as legal advice, financial advice, investment advice, public-policy advice, administrative eligibility advice, or empirical claims about real households, workers, consumers, agencies, firms, platforms, communities, or institutions.
