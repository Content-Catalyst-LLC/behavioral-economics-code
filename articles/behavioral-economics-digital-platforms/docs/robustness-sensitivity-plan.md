# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Professional platform-economics workflows should include robustness checks.

## Recommended checks

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare OLS, logit/probit for binary outcomes, and welfare regressions.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by cognitive-overload quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by privacy-sensitivity quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by digital-literacy quartile.
6. Test alternative privacy-cost weights.
7. Test alternative attention-cost weights.
8. Test alternative platform-value weights.
9. Compare user welfare, platform value, and welfare gap.
10. Evaluate whether concentration rises without welfare improvement.
11. Report standard errors and confidence intervals where packages are available.

## Placebo checks for real data

- Pre-treatment engagement.
- Pre-treatment item-category preferences.
- Prior account age or baseline usage.
- Pre-treatment exposure concentration.
- Baseline privacy settings.

## External validity

Real platform environments differ by product category, market power, switching costs, user demographics, regulatory setting, and monetization model. Synthetic results should not be interpreted as real-world platform estimates.
