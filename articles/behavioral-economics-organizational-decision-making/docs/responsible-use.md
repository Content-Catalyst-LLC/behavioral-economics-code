# Responsible Use

This article scaffold supports synthetic-data research and reproducible learning about behavioral economics in organizational decision-making.

Appropriate uses include:

- public learning
- synthetic scenario analysis
- computational methods demonstration
- organizational governance research
- reproducible documentation practice
- institutional learning about decision processes

Inappropriate uses include:

- employee screening
- hiring, promotion, compensation, discipline, or termination decisions
- workplace surveillance
- individual performance scoring
- psychological profiling
- automated worker management
- coercive behavioral manipulation
- production deployment without legal, ethical, and empirical review

Organizational behavioral research should protect worker voice, procedural fairness, transparency, accountability, and institutional learning.
