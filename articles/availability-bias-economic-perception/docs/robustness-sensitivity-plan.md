# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare subjective probability and calibration error separately.
3. Compare behavior change and welfare proxy separately.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by availability-sensitivity quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by numeracy quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by trust-in-statistics quartile.
7. Estimate heterogeneous effects by risk-tolerance quartile.
8. Estimate heterogeneous effects by prior-experience status.
9. Vary salience-scale assumptions.
10. Vary base-rate-disclosure assumptions.
11. Vary emotional-intensity assumptions.
12. Vary welfare weights on calibration, comprehension, and emotional burden.
13. Test whether salience raises concern without improving calibration.
14. Test whether base-rate disclosure improves calibration among low-trust groups.

## External validity

Real-world availability effects depend on media exposure, platform design, trust, prior experience, statistical literacy, emotional intensity, group vulnerability, local risk, source credibility, and whether a salient event is representative or genuinely informative.
