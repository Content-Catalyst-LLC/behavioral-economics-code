# Responsible Use

Appropriate uses include behavioral-economics teaching, risk-perception research, policy-evaluation scaffolding, investor-sentiment analysis, insurance-demand modeling, crisis-communication review, climate-risk communication, digital-platform governance, public-interest institutional learning, and synthetic simulation.

Inappropriate uses include fear-based persuasion, panic amplification, manipulative risk messaging, deceptive marketing, sensationalized engagement design, financial product manipulation, public-policy scare tactics, or using salience to bypass informed judgment.

All outputs are synthetic. They should not be interpreted as legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, investment advice, product recommendations, or empirical claims about real investors, households, platform users, patients, voters, communities, or public audiences.
