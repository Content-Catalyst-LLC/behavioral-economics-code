# Calibration and Welfare Notes

Availability-bias interventions should be evaluated by calibration, comprehension, autonomy, and welfare, not attention alone.

A stylized welfare expression is:

```text
Welfare = Decision Quality + Calibration + Comprehension + Autonomy - Distortion - Emotional Burden
```

## Core welfare questions

- Does the communication make risk perception more accurate?
- Does it provide base rates or only vivid examples?
- Does it help people distinguish salience from frequency?
- Does it raise concern in proportion to evidence?
- Does it create panic, fatalism, stigma, or distrust?
- Does it make hidden structural risks visible?
- Are vulnerable groups protected from fear-based manipulation?
- Does the institution benefit from making a risk more available?

## Recommended diagnostics

- Subjective probability by regime.
- Calibration error by regime.
- Risky-asset participation by regime.
- Insurance demand by regime.
- Policy support by regime.
- Welfare proxy by regime.
- Effects by availability-sensitivity quartile.
- Effects by numeracy quartile.
- Effects by trust-in-statistics quartile.
- Sensitivity to salience, base-rate disclosure, and emotional intensity.
