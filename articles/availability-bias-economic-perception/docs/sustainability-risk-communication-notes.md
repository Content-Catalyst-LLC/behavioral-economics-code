# Sustainability and Climate Risk Communication Notes

Availability bias matters for sustainability because many environmental risks are slow, diffuse, cumulative, and difficult to perceive directly.

## Availability challenges

- Climate trends unfold over decades.
- Biodiversity loss may be invisible until thresholds are crossed.
- Soil degradation and water stress can be gradual.
- Pollution accumulation may lack dramatic event form.
- Infrastructure fragility is often hidden until failure.
- Future generations cannot make risk vivid in present politics.

## Availability opportunities

- Local disaster examples can make abstract risk concrete.
- Health co-benefits can make sustainability more immediate.
- Public dashboards can show slow trends.
- Community monitoring can make hidden harms visible.
- Scenario planning can make future risk imaginable.
- Story plus evidence can improve comprehension.

## Cautions

- Disaster-only framing can produce fatigue or helplessness.
- Green claims can become availability-driven greenwashing.
- Marginalized communities should not be used merely as vivid examples.
- Structural risk should be connected to agency, policy, and justice.
