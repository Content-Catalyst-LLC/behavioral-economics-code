# Digital Platform Attention Notes

Digital platforms can amplify availability bias by repeatedly surfacing emotionally intense, vivid, recent, and engaging examples.

## Platform mechanisms

- Trending lists.
- Recommendation feeds.
- Push notifications.
- Viral videos.
- Search ranking.
- Algorithmic personalization.
- High-arousal content promotion.
- Social proof counters.
- Repost cascades.
- Short clips without context.
- Market dashboards highlighting recent movement.
- Review systems that emphasize memorable outliers.

## User-aligned countermeasures

- Base-rate context.
- Representative summaries.
- Distributional views.
- Historical comparisons.
- Source-quality indicators.
- Repetition controls.
- Risk denominators.
- Total frequency and incidence displays.
- Friction before sharing unverified crisis content.
- Long-term trend panels for slow risks.

Whoever controls attention helps control perceived probability. Platform design should make that power accountable.
