# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Professional choice-architecture workflows should include robustness checks.

## Recommended checks

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare OLS models with multinomial or logit/probit specifications where appropriate.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by complexity-sensitivity quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by default-sensitivity quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by digital-literacy quartile.
6. Test alternative cognitive-cost weights.
7. Test alternative switching-cost weights.
8. Test alternative default-strength assumptions.
9. Compare behavioral effects with welfare effects.
10. Test whether guided design improves welfare more than default-heavy design.
11. Report standard errors and confidence intervals where packages are available.

## Placebo checks for real data

- Pre-treatment option selection.
- Baseline digital literacy.
- Prior platform use.
- Pre-treatment default status.
- Prior enrollment or switching history.

## External validity

Real choice architecture effects depend on domain, stakes, user population, trust, institutional legitimacy, complexity, digital access, and available alternatives. Synthetic results should not be interpreted as real-world estimates.
