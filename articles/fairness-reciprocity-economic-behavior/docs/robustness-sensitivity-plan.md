# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without covariates.
2. Compare OLS with logit/probit for binary rejection and punishment outcomes.
3. Estimate heterogeneous effects by fairness-sensitivity quartile.
4. Estimate heterogeneous effects by reciprocity-sensitivity quartile.
5. Estimate heterogeneous effects by trust quartile.
6. Estimate heterogeneous effects by punishment-willingness quartile.
7. Vary process-fairness weights.
8. Vary reciprocity-signal weights.
9. Vary rejection-cost assumptions.
10. Vary punishment-cost assumptions.
11. Compare material-payoff rankings with fairness-reciprocity welfare rankings.
12. Report standard errors and confidence intervals where packages are available.

## External validity

Real-world fairness and reciprocity depend on institutions, labor-market power, legal context, culture, history, class, race, gender, organizational rank, platform power, and beliefs about intention, merit, and legitimacy.
