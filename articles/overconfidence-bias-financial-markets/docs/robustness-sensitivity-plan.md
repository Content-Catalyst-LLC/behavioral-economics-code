# Robustness and Sensitivity Plan

Recommended checks:

1. Estimate treatment effects with and without friction and leverage controls.
2. Compare gross and net realized returns.
3. Compare trading-cost drag by overconfidence regime.
4. Vary overconfidence multipliers.
5. Vary trading-friction assumptions.
6. Vary leverage-access assumptions.
7. Vary true signal-quality dispersion.
8. Vary prior-success sensitivity.
9. Vary diversification-discipline assumptions.
10. Estimate heterogeneity by risk-tolerance quartile.
11. Estimate heterogeneity by signal-quality quartile.
12. Compare regime outcomes under bull, neutral, and adverse market-return assumptions.
13. Decompose realized performance into beta/factor/skill/noise components.

## External validity

Real-world overconfidence depends on investor experience, platform design, market regime, product access, leverage, social information, financial literacy, liquidity needs, tax considerations, and institutional constraints.
