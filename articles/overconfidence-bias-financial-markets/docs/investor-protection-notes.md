# Investor-Protection Notes

Overconfidence becomes harmful when investors translate fragile confidence into excessive turnover, leverage, product complexity, concentration, or poor timing.

## Key diagnostics

- Turnover relative to benchmark or stated strategy.
- Trading cost as share of gross return.
- Realized return after costs.
- Risk-adjusted performance.
- Position concentration.
- Leverage exposure.
- Sensitivity to prior success.
- Forecast calibration.
- Performance attribution to beta, factors, skill, and noise.
- Friction and salience in the trading interface.

## Governance relevance

Investor-protection analysis should distinguish:

- financial access from harmful frictionless speculation
- real skill from lucky short-term performance
- disclosure from genuine comprehension
- action from control
- confidence from calibration
- engagement from welfare
